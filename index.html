Giving Advice & Recommendations

Master the 5 structural levels of delivering strategic career advice in executive contexts.

2nd Conditional Hypothetical / Soft
If + Subject + Past Simple, Subject + WOULD + Verb

Used for advisory distancing ("If I were you...").

"If I were in your position, I would go for a career in IT."

'd Better (Had Better) Urgent / Warning
Subject + 'D BETTER + Bare Infinitive

Strong advice with potential negative consequences if ignored.

"You'd better analyze your weaknesses before switching roles."

Imperative Direct / Actionable
[Base Verb] + Direct Object / Action

Direct action-oriented directives common in coaching & leadership.

"Play to your strengths and address employer challenges."

Modal Verbs Standard / Professional
Subject + SHOULD / OUGHT TO + Base Verb

Standard recommendation level in professional conversations.

"You should look for a role you feel passionate about."

Coaching Question Socratic / Consultative
WHY DON'T YOU + Base Verb...?

Invites self-reflection without forcing a direction.

"Why don't you consider relocating to boost growth?"
