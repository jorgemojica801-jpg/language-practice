RULE 1 Career Objectives & Methods

Gerund (-ing) vs. Infinitive (to + verb)

In business speech, we use the **Gerund** to talk about ongoing methods, systems, or habitual practices, and the **Infinitive** to express specific purpose or future goals.

METHOD / PROCESS (GERUND)

"Upskilling involves dedicating 15 minutes daily to reading publications."

Structure: Verb + Verb-ING (involve, consider, advocate)

PURPOSE / AIM (INFINITIVE)

"To avoid deskilling, professionals strive to master new tools."

Structure: Verb + TO + Base Verb (strive, decide, prepare)
