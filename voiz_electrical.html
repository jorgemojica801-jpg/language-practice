Automotive Diagnostic English Grammar Framework

Instructions for VOIZ Vehicle Diagnostic Sprints

Diagnosing high-end vehicle faults requires rapid, logical communication with team leads, master technicians, and customers. Use this 3-step grammar system to answer diagnostic prompts in under 10 seconds:

1. State System Status (Present)

"Multiple ECUs communicate across the CAN network."

2. State Findings (Past)

"The scanner read fault code P0300 from the engine ECU."

3. State Diagnostic Action (Must/Future)

"I must test voltage and clear codes after repair."

1. Present Simple

System Functions & Tools

Used to describe how ECUs operate, what diagnostic tools do, and continuous electrical signals.

(+) The ECU manages the braking system.

(+) Oscilloscopes display live signals.

(-) The scanner does not replace parts.

(?) Do you compare expected values?

2. Past Simple

Customer Reports & Findings

Used to describe when fault symptoms started, what the visual inspection revealed, and recorded measurements.

(+) The warning light appeared yesterday.

(+) I found corrosion on the ground wire.

(-) The multimeter didn't show voltage.

(?) Did the customer explain symptoms?

3. Imperatives, Modals & Future

Diagnostic Procedures & Repairs

Used for mandatory diagnostic steps, clearing fault codes, calibrating sensors, and road testing.

(+) Technicians must clear fault codes.

(+) I am going to test one possibility.

(-) Do not replace expensive parts.

(?) Will you road test the vehicle?
