EFL Grammar Framework

Grammar in Technical Context

Domina las 4 estructuras clave usadas en talleres y reportes técnicos: Present Simple, Past Simple, Future Simple y "Used to".

Habits & Technical Facts

Present Simple

Se usa para rutinas, hechos universales y funcionamiento permanente de componentes.

[+] Subject + Verb (s/es) + Object
[-] Subject + do/does not + Verb + Object
[?] Do/Does + Subject + Verb + Object?
PAST PRESENT (PERMANENT) FUTURE
Example: "The alternator charges the battery when the engine runs."
Completed Actions

Past Simple

Se usa para reparaciones finalizadas, inspecciones o eventos en un momento específico del pasado.

[+] Subject + Verb (-ed / irregular) + Object
[-] Subject + did not + Verb (base) + Object
[?] Did + Subject + Verb (base) + Object?
FINISHED PAST PRESENT FUTURE
Example: "The mechanic replaced the spark plugs yesterday morning."
Plans & Predictions

Future Simple

Usamos Will para decisiones/promesas inmediatas y Going to para planes o servicios programados.

[Will] Subj + will + Verb + Object
[Going to] Subj + am/is/are + going to + Verb
PAST PRESENT FUTURE
Example: "We are going to install a new radiator tomorrow."
Past Habits / Contrast

Expression: "Used to"

Se usa para hábitos o estados del pasado que ya no ocurren en el presente.

[+] Subject + used to + Verb (base)
[-] Subject + didn't use to + Verb (base)
PAST HABIT (STOPPED) NOW (DIFFERENT)
Contrast with Past Simple: "I used to drive an old truck, but yesterday I bought a modern EV."
