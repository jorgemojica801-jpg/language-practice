Structural Formulas

Passive Voice Formula

Subject + [To Be] + Past Participle (+ by Agent)

Example: "The film was shot in 1997."

Active Contrast

Subject + Action Verb + Direct Object

Example: "The director shot the film in 1997."

Temporal Framing Timeline

PAST ACTION (Completed)

"The movie was filmed in New Zealand."

Focus on the production process that finished in the past.

PRESENT STATE (Permanent/Continuous)

"The movie is set in a fictional universe."

Focus on narrative background, state, or setting today.
