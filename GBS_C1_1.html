Interactive

Look at these three common verbs used in professional growth. Can you explain what sets them apart?

upskill

Learn new skills or teach workers new skills to do their current job better.

reskill

Learn completely new skills so you can do a different job or role.

deskill

Reduce the level of skill needed to carry out a job, often due to automation.

DEFINITION

To learn additional skills or enhance existing skills to adapt to new workplace demands.

EXAMPLE

"Our company provides software training so engineers can upskill."

Verb

reskill

Click to reveal definition

2 / 3
DEFINITION

To train or learn entirely new skills in order to qualify for a completely different role or career.

EXAMPLE

"Many traditional bank tellers had to reskill into digital support roles."

Verb

deskill

Click to reveal definition

3 / 3
DEFINITION

To automate or simplify a job so much that workers no longer need specialized knowledge.

EXAMPLE

"Modern software deskilled assembly work by automating calculation steps."

3 Alternative Ways to Upskill

1

10-15 Min Daily Habits: Dedicate 10 to 15 minutes a day reading industry publications, listening to podcasts, or watching TED talks.

2

Reverse Mentorship: Find a younger or junior employee with stronger digital skills to mentor senior team members.

3

Industry Reminders: Set scheduled reminders to read up on market developments and initiate conversations.

Vote recorded!
