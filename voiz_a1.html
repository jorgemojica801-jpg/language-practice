A1 Grammar Reference & Quick Guide

Essential tenses needed for the VOIZ 10-second oral sprint workouts.

1. Present Simple

Habits & Routines

Used for facts, daily routines, and permanent states.

(+) I/You/We/They eat breakfast.

(+) He/She/It eats at 8 AM.

(-) I don't work on Sundays.

(?) Do you like coffee?

2. Past Simple

Completed Actions

Used for actions finished at a specific time in the past.

(+) Regular: I cooked yesterday.

(+) Irregular: I went home early.

(-) She didn't call me.

(?) Did you watch TV last night?

3. Future Simple

Plans & Predictions

Used with will (spontaneous/promises) or be going to (plans).

(+) I will travel next week.

(+) I am going to buy a car.

(-) He won't come tonight.

(?) Will you study English later?

How to Use VOIZ for Max Speaking Fluency
