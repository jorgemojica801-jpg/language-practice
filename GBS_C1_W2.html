Grammar & Functional Language in Practice

Master key structures used for professional career growth, hypotheticals, recruitment debate, and decision making.

Rule 1: Conditionals & Advice

Second & Third Conditionals

Used to give advice, express hypothetical scenarios in career planning, or reflect on past hiring methods.

2nd Conditional: If + Past Simple, ... would + Verb
"If I were to give advice, I would focus on upskilling."
3rd / Past Reflection: If + Past Perfect / Past Simple ... had to
"Think back to 10 years ago when we had to sift through resumes manually."

Rule 2: Professional Verb Patterns

Gerunds & Infinitives

Specific verbs in corporate contexts dictate gerund (`-ing`) or infinitive (`to + v`) complements.

dedicate time to + -ING:
"Dedicate 15 minutes a day to reading industry updates."
help + (to) Verb / eliminate:
"AI tools help eliminate bias in screening."
Key High-Frequency Verbs
consider + -ing allow to + v focus on + -ing
Rule 3: Contrast & Transition

Nuanced Professional Contrast

Expressing shifts between concepts (e.g., upskilling vs reskilling, or manual vs AI hiring).

Shift: moving from [A] into [B]
"We are moving from routine work into knowledge work."
Concession: While [X] might seem..., it can also...
"While job hopping might seem negative, it can indicate adaptability."
