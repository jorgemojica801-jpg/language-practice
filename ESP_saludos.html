Time-of-Day Greetings Breakdown

Interactive clock selector based on Section 4 of the PDF worksheet.

09:00 AM Buenos días
Morning 6:00 AM - 11:59 AM

Buenos días

Used from morning sunrise until noon.

Ex: "Buenos días, señor Miyamoto."
Afternoon 12:00 PM - 5:59 PM

Buenas tardes

Used from noon until dusk / early evening.

Ex: "Buenas tardes, señora Pérez."
Night / Evening 6:00 PM - 5:59 AM

Buenas noches

Used for both greeting at night and wishing good night before sleep.

Ex: "Buenas noches, Pedro. Descansa."
